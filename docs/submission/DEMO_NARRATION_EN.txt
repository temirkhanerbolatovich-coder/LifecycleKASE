This is LifecycleKASE, a corporate-action terminal for tokenized bonds. In this demo, we review a completed run on a disposable Solana local network. It is a prototype, not a production financial service.

The test instrument starts with thirty-five bonds held by three investors. The workflow separates issuer preparation from approval. Each financial operation uses a saved transaction, an explicit signer, and finalized confirmation.

For the coupon, the finalized holder snapshot is nine, twenty-one, and five bonds. Entitlements are four hundred fifty, one thousand fifty, and two hundred fifty K Z T test: seventeen hundred fifty in total. The effective ownership cutoff is the finalized capture slot and time. We do not reconstruct balances at an earlier planned timestamp.

The full amount is reserved before execution. Confirmed transfers and a final receipt establish the coupon result. The bond supply stays at thirty-five. This is an on-chain test-token transfer, not a real fiat payment.

Next, twenty percent partial redemption pays seven thousand K Z T test and burns seven bonds. The historical supply changes from thirty-five to twenty-eight. Payment and burn share one transaction. If an on-chain leg fails, both legs roll back.

At maturity, the remaining twenty-eight bonds receive twenty-nine thousand four hundred K Z T test, including the final coupon. All remaining bonds are burned. Supply reaches zero and the instrument becomes redeemed.

Each action produces a versioned canonical JSON receipt. Its exact SHA two fifty-six hash is committed to a Solana receipt account. Cash, token effects, and finalized signatures are reconciled before database projection. An unknown confirmation requires checking the original signature, not submitting a replacement payment.

Funding and eligibility are simulated. Public-network deployment, real K Y C, fiat settlement, and exchange integration remain future work. The repository contains the saved evidence and instructions to reproduce this local run.
