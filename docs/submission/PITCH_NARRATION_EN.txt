Tokenizing a bond is only the beginning. The issuer still needs to identify holders, calculate what they are owed, approve the budget, pay them, and prove what happened. LifecycleKASE brings that post-issuance workflow into one operator terminal.

Our starting hypothesis is a narrow one: operators servicing test tokenized bonds need a consistent path from holder records to a verifiable action result. We focus on coupon payments, partial early redemption, and redemption at maturity.

The prototype combines a web terminal, a wallet-authenticated API, PostgreSQL, and a Solana program. A finalized snapshot fixes entitlements. A separate approver and full reserve precede execution. For redemption, payment and token burn happen atomically. The final receipt hash is committed on-chain.

Our completed local test pays seventeen hundred fifty K Z T test in coupon, redeems twenty percent for seven thousand, then settles maturity for twenty-nine thousand four hundred. The remaining token supply reaches zero. These are reproducible technical results, not customer traction or real fiat settlement.
