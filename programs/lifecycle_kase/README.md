# Anchor program: issuance, calculation and isolated corporate-action settlement

The **2026-10-12 controlled-registry candidate** adds a new instrument v2/default-frozen mint, program-mediated transfers, complete locked capture, per-source unit reservations and atomic thaw/burn/refreeze around shared settlement. Known holder CPI Guard/MemoTransfer extensions cannot strand assigned burns. Existing layouts and instrument v1 paths are preserved. See [protocol and supported instructions](../../docs/features/controlled-registry.md), [ADR-030](../../docs/decisions/ADR-030-controlled-registry-and-unit-reservations.md) and [dated verification](../../docs/testing/controlled-registry-2026-10-12.md). API/database/UI and owner/Devnet rollout remain separate. The initialization, activation, cancellation and snapshot descriptions below concern the legacy instrument v1 path; controlled issuance mints its supply during its own bootstrap.

Current source/acceptance boundaries: [implemented versus simulated](../../docs/IMPLEMENTED_VS_SIMULATED.md), [controlled protocol](../../docs/testing/controlled-registry-2026-10-12.md) and [controlled application](../../docs/testing/controlled-workflow-integration-2026-10-12.md). The October 10 audit is a historical checkpoint.

The current candidate also includes exact-target largest-remainder v2 and a new controlled instrument v2 with default-frozen mints, PDA freeze authority, authorized transfers, locked complete raw capture and assigned-unit reservation/settlement. See [ADR-029](../../docs/decisions/ADR-029-exact-target-redemption.md), [ADR-030](../../docs/decisions/ADR-030-controlled-registry-and-unit-reservations.md) and [controlled contract](../../docs/features/controlled-registry.md). Earlier initialization descriptions below concern legacy token policy. The accepted controlled four-action runtime has separately fingerprinted SBF/IDL/source; owner/Public Devnet installation remains open.

The Localnet candidate implements entitlement registration/finalization/reset, separate approver/reserve, coupon payment and [atomic early/maturity redemption](../../docs/features/redemption-execution-and-receipts.md). Redemption adds immutable source and supply-progress PDAs without reallocating the existing Instrument, Action or Entitlement layouts. Exact payment and PermanentDelegate burns share one transaction and receipt; zero final supply retires the instrument. Current isolated runtime proof is in [three-action acceptance](../../docs/testing/kase-three-actions-2026-10-10.md). The retained owner registration candidate was upgraded on October9; RESET and three REGISTER were accepted, while FINALIZE remains unresolved. This newer settlement candidate is not installed on that ledger.

`initialize_instrument` creates the instrument PDA at `["instrument", instrument UUID bytes]` in `Deploying` status. The signer must be the current upgrade authority of this program (verified against its own ProgramData account) and becomes the instrument's issuer authority. The supplied bond and settlement mints must be distinct Token-2022 mints with 0 and 6 decimals respectively. The bond mint must have the requested nonzero supply, no mint or freeze authority, and the Instrument Authority PDA as its permanent delegate. Terms also validate the face value, coupon rate, payment frequency, dates, authorities, and UUID.

`create_corporate_action` creates `["action", instrument PDA, action UUID bytes]` in `Scheduled` status. Only the instrument's issuer authority may sign. It rejects a nil action ID, past or out-of-order dates, paused/redeemed instruments, and parameters that do not match coupon, maturity redemption, or early redemption. Coupon and early redemption must execute before or at maturity as applicable; maturity redemption must execute at or after maturity. Scheduling is allowed while the instrument is `Deploying` so preparation can precede activation; snapshot registration requires `Active`; coupon and redemption execution require separate approval and reserve. Snapshot fields and counters begin at zero.

`activate_instrument` moves an instrument from `Deploying` to `Active` only when its issuer signs and 1–64 distinct, positive-balance Token-2022 holder accounts for the bond mint sum exactly to its supply at activation. It rechecks the mint configuration and permanent delegate. This is on-chain balance reconciliation, **not** KYC or verified investor-wallet mapping; the application must verify those separately and confirm the activation transaction at `finalized`. See [ADR-009](../../docs/decisions/ADR-009-instrument-activation.md).

`cancel_action` lets the issuer authority move only a `Scheduled` action to terminal `Cancelled`, recording the terminal timestamp. It checks the action's instrument relationship and PDA seeds. A database-only `Draft` is cancelled without an on-chain transaction. Actions after snapshot registration are not cancellable through this instruction.

`register_snapshot` lets the instrument issuer commit a nonzero snapshot hash, a past-or-current slot and investor/wallet/balance counts to a `Scheduled` action on an `Active` instrument. The on-chain Clock must be within 300 seconds after `record_at`, and total balance must equal the live Token-2022 bond mint supply. Registration changes the action to `SnapshotCreated`; the commitment cannot be replaced. This does **not** prove the hash preimage, verified investor mapping, RPC finality, or block time. See [ADR-010](../../docs/decisions/ADR-010-snapshot-registration.md).

These instructions do **not** issue or distribute settlement tokens or verify investor identities. Entitlement registration/reset/finalization is installed in the historically accepted owner candidate. Separate approver/reserve, coupon execution/finalization and atomic early/maturity redemption instructions exist in the newer isolated candidate; its [three-action runtime acceptance](../../docs/testing/kase-three-actions-2026-10-10.md) is separate from retained-owner installation/payment/receipt acceptance and public Devnet deployment, which remain open. The MVP binds administrator access to the program's upgrade authority; an immutable program or a different issuer wallet requires a separate governance design before deployment. See [ADR-008](../../docs/decisions/ADR-008-program-administrator.md).

## Local development

From Ubuntu WSL, in the repository root:

```bash
cargo fmt --all -- --check
cargo test -p lifecycle_kase --locked
cargo clippy -p lifecycle_kase --all-targets --locked -- -D warnings
anchor build
```

After `anchor build`, run `npm ci --prefix tools/solana-integration` and `npm run test:solana:integration` from PowerShell. This loads the program as upgradeable into an isolated local validator's genesis with a disposable upgrade-authority key and tests instrument initialization, action scheduling/cancellation, activation with a 10/20/5 distribution, and snapshot commitment/replay rejection. No persistent program keypair is needed for this test. To prove a candidate upgrade against state created by the retained binary, run `scripts/test-initialize-instrument.ps1 -Profile localnet -UpgradeCandidateProfile localnet-candidate-reset-20261008`; the harness performs the real loader upgrade and compares an existing Action PDA byte-for-byte before continuing the candidate suite. Use `-WslUser lifecycle-dev -Profile devnet` to test the isolated Devnet-profile artifacts instead; see the [build procedure](../../docs/deployment/devnet-build.md). The snapshot hash in this test is synthetic; canonical `snapshot-v2` content remains covered by separate domain tests. The test does not use or create a Devnet key or verify a public deployment transaction. Its legacy Anchor client dependencies are isolated from the deployed application; see the [toolchain notes](../../docs/development/toolchain.md) for the current security limitation.

The default program ID is a disposable localnet address; the existing localnet keypair was exposed in a diagnostic and must not be used for Devnet or real assets. A separate `devnet` feature selects the approved fresh public identity without copying its private key into the checkout. Use the [isolated Devnet build procedure](../../docs/deployment/devnet-build.md) and its source/IDL/plan/artifact checks. A fresh checkout needs a disposable local keypair for local deployment; align only the localnet declaration/mapping after reviewing that key's public address. Do not blindly run `anchor keys sync` across conditional identities, commit any keypair, or deploy with the default localnet key against Devnet. The build procedure does not deploy the program.

## Zero-rounded coupon candidate — 2026-10-11

Zero payment amounts are registered with the existing NotEligibleZeroRounding status, matching the API. FINALIZE rejects a total of zero. Registered zero rows still count toward snapshot coverage but cannot execute payment; mixed final receipts preserve them without a settlement. Partial all-zero registration can use the existing complete-set reset before finalization. Account layout and instruction signatures are unchanged.

The newly built disposable candidate passed runtime mixed 1/2/0 and all-zero regressions and all three action flows; see [candidate acceptance](../../docs/testing/submission-readiness-2026-10-11.md). It does not replace the accepted owner artifact or install itself on a retained/public ledger.
